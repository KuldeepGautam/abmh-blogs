Muscular Dystrophy Treatment and Diagnosis

Muscular dystrophy is assessed by understanding how muscle strength changes over time and identifying the underlying cause. In Duchenne muscular dystrophy, early diagnosis is essential for guiding supportive care and long-term management.

What muscular dystrophy diagnosis may include?

When muscle weakness or delayed development is observed, doctors assess patterns in strength and movement to confirm Duchenne muscular dystrophy or other types.

  • Clinical assessment: Evaluates muscle strength, gait, and developmental milestones
  • Blood tests: Detect elevated creatine kinase (CK) levels
  • Genetic testing: Confirms DMD and identifies mutations in the dystrophin gene
  • Functional evaluation: Assesses mobility, balance, and daily function

What are the types of Duchenne muscular dystrophy?

While Duchenne muscular dystrophy is the most common severe form, other types vary in onset and progression.

  • Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD): Begins in early childhood and progresses faster
  • Becker muscular dystrophy: Milder, with later onset
  • Congenital muscular dystrophy: Present at birth
  • Other rare types: Differ in severity and progression

Causes and symptoms of Duchenne muscular dystrophy

Understanding causes and symptoms helps in early recognition and timely care.

  • Cause: Genetic mutation affecting dystrophin, a protein essential for muscle strength
  • Inheritance: X-linked, primarily affecting boys
  • Early symptoms: Difficulty running, climbing, or rising (Gowers’ sign)
  • Progressive weakness: Gradual muscle loss over time
  • Movement changes: Frequent falls or reduced stamina

Muscular dystrophy treatment and management

While there is no cure, treatment focuses on slowing progression and improving quality of life.

  • Treatment plans: Long-term, supportive, and individualised
  • Medications: May include corticosteroids to slow muscle degeneration
  • Rehabilitation: Supports mobility, posture, and strength
  • Supportive care: Includes respiratory and cardiac monitoring
  • Monitoring: Adjusts care as the condition progresses

FAQs on Muscular Dystrophy

1. What is Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD)?

DMD is a genetic condition causing progressive muscle weakness, usually beginning in early childhood and requiring long-term care.

2. What are the early symptoms of Duchenne muscular dystrophy?

Early signs include difficulty running, climbing stairs, frequent falls, or trouble getting up from the floor.

3. What causes Duchenne muscular dystrophy?

It is caused by a mutation in the dystrophin gene, affecting muscle strength, and is more common in boys.

4. What treatment options are available for DMD?

Treatment includes medications, physiotherapy, and supportive care to manage symptoms and improve quality of life.

5. Is there a cure for Duchenne muscular dystrophy?

There is no cure, but early and ongoing care can help slow progression and manage symptoms.

6. How can families support someone with DMD?

By following care plans, encouraging safe activity, attending regular check-ups, and adapting daily routines.
